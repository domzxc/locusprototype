Please find attached my approach document for the Manila FMCG fleet-allocation and dispatch case study.

- **Approach document:** DomTransport-Approach.pdf (attached)
- **GitHub source:** https://github.com/domzxc/locusprototype
- **Working application:** https://domtransport.onrender.com

DomTransport compares normal-day and peak-season fleet requirements across the six distributors, with Pasig as the main delivery demonstration. It includes synthetic orders, separate frozen/dry deliveries, CBM/KG capacity checks, retailer receiving hours, crew availability, road-based dispatch validation, visible service gaps and operating-cost assumptions.

All operational data is synthetic. Traffic delays and cost rates are declared assumptions, GPS playback is simulated, and the planner is a heuristic rather than a proven optimizer. The document distinguishes the implemented prototype from the proposed forecasting and optimization models for real historical data.

I look forward to presenting the approach, demonstrating the application and discussing the assumptions and tradeoffs with the panel.
